# Findings register

Sprint 1 security review (lane S1), 9 November 2026. Scope and attacker models: `THREAT-MODEL.md`.
Evidence for every probe cited here is under `evidence/sprint-1/S1/`
(`probe-manifest.mjs`, `probe-manifest.out`, `static-checks.txt`, `pins.txt`), with the fix-round
re-verifications under `round2/` and `round3/`.

Severity scale: Critical (exploitable now, breaks the core promise), High (exploitable with
realistic preconditions), Medium (design gap or parity break with security impact), Low
(hardening, availability, defence in depth), Info (observation, no action required). Status:
Open (needs a fix), Accepted (recorded risk with a target sprint), Mitigated (control landed;
residual noted), Noted (information only).

evidence under `evidence/sprint-1/S1/round3/` and the tester folders cited per row):
